---

## Estimation Approach: ±40% Bands

Never provide single-point estimates. Always use ranges with ±40% variance.

**Formula:**
```
Midpoint = (hours/week × weeks × rate)
Low = Midpoint × 0.6
High = Midpoint × 1.4
```

## Example: Building a Range

**Scenario:** 10-week advisory engagement, standard intensity

```
SA: 18 hrs/wk × 10 weeks × $335/hr = $60,300 (midpoint)
SDM: 4 hrs/wk × 10 weeks × $260/hr = $10,400 (midpoint)
Combined midpoint: $70,700

±40% band:
  Low:  $70,700 × 0.6 = ~$42K
  High: $70,700 × 1.4 = ~$99K

Present as: "$45K - $100K"
(Round to clean numbers for professional presentation)
```
